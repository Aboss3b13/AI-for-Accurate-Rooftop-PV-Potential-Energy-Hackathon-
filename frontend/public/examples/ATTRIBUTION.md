# Swiss aerial examples

Original imagery: SWISSIMAGE / Federal Office of Topography swisstopo.
Source dataset: Jean Perbet, [Swiss solar panels segmentation](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jeanprbt/swiss-solar-panels-segmentation), version 1.
Kaggle metadata lists the dataset as **CC0: Public Domain**. Attribution is retained here and in the interface.

These are cropped real aerial images, not generated pictures. See `manifest.json` for original filenames and `scripts/bundle_examples.py` for reproducible crops. Roof boundaries and the obstacle-case marks were manually prepared for interaction demonstrations. They are not ground truth for capacity evaluation and are not presented as AI predictions.
